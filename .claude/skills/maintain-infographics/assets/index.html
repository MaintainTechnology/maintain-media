Logo

The Maintain Audits wordmark. Keep clear space ≥ the cap height on all sides; minimum width 120px.

Files: assets/logo/wordmark-on-dark.svg · wordmark-on-light.svg. Don't stretch or add effects. On imagery (fourth tile) the light wordmark sits over a ~50% Forge Blue scrim so it never fights the terrain behind it.

Iconography

14 line icons at 24×24, stroke: currentColor, width 2, round caps. Use via sprite.svg; don't mix in another set.

Signature backgrounds

The brand texture: a 1px green grid, a soft green glow, and the two combined for hero surfaces. Utilities live in tokens.css (.ma-grid-bg, .ma-glow).
