# ABR Lead Engine — primary-source checks

Checked 8 September 2026 against the original v3.5 specification. This is an independent factual check, not a legal opinion or approval to contact anyone. No accounts were opened, contact lists washed, messages sent, or third parties contacted. The canonical specification was not edited by this reviewer.

## Confirmed corrections

| ID | Finding and correction | Primary source checked |
|---|---|---|
| SRC-01 | **GST registration does not prove actual turnover above $75,000.** Registration can be voluntary below the threshold, can follow projected turnover, and is compulsory for taxi/ride-sourcing irrespective of turnover. Replace “just crossed”, “has revenue >$75k”, and similar claims throughout Objective, R14/tiering and Appendix with “observed GST registration change; a hypothesis to test, not verified sales or growth”. Never populate estimated revenue from GST alone. | [ATO: when GST registration is needed](https://community.ato.gov.au/s/article/a07RF00000ekjCh/when-you-need-to-register-for-gst); [ATO: registration and current/projected turnover](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst). The main ATO page returned HTTP 403 on direct open; its official indexed content and the ATO Community article were available. |
| SRC-02 | **QBCC financial categories are not actual audited revenue bands.** Category 1 covers maximum-revenue capacity $800,001–$3,000,000; Category 2 covers $3,000,001–$12,000,000. The category is a public range; the licence's specific maximum revenue is a separate amount within that range. Replace “regulator-verified revenue”, “has revenue $800k–$12m”, and “businesses in the revenue band” with “licence financial category indicating permitted revenue capacity; actual turnover unknown”. It can remain an ICP proxy. | [QBCC: maximum revenue](https://qbcc.qld.gov.au/running-your-business/financial-requirements/maximum-revenue) |
| SRC-03 | **R25's claim that the DNCR generally accepts business numbers since 2010 is false.** Eligible telephone numbers are primarily private/domestic; exclusively fax or government numbers have separate eligibility. Business-publication does not establish the primary use of a mobile. Keep the conservative policy to wash every phone; remove the wrong legal explanation. A clear wash is one prerequisite, not a complete permission to call. | [DNCR: registering numbers](https://www.donotcall.gov.au/Consumers/Consumer-Overview/Registering-numbers) |
| SRC-04 | **Direct automated washing materially exceeds the presumed low-volume cost.** Both SFTP and SOAP require subscription **D or above**. The published table lists B: 20,000 credits/$126; D: 1,000,000 credits/$5,058. The fee page labels these the rates effective 1 October 2021 and still displays them at review. Resolve the architecture now: MVP manual web upload and validated receipt import on B; or separately price/select a third-party API; or explicitly budget D. Account lead time remains unverified. Count repeated washes as credits too. | [DNCR subscriptions](https://www.donotcall.gov.au/Industry/Subscription-Overview), [SFTP access requirements](https://www.donotcall.gov.au/industry/washing-process-overview/sftp), [SOAP access requirements](https://www.donotcall.gov.au/industry/washing-process-overview/soap) |
| SRC-05 | **R28 confuses telemarketing and door-to-door windows.** ACCC distinguishes weekday telephone calls 9am–8pm from door visits 9am–6pm; Saturday is 9am–5pm for both, with no Sundays/public holidays. The 6pm limit may be retained as a stricter business policy, but must not be attributed to closing a sale by telephone. Store recipient timezone and enforce the approved policy immediately before a call; ambiguous locality/timezone should block rather than guess. | [ACCC: telemarketing and door-to-door sales](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/telemarketing-and-door-to-door-sales), [ACMA: telemarketing rules](https://www.acma.gov.au/say-no-to-telemarketers) |
| SRC-06 | **R24a's blanket APP 7.3 conclusion omits APP 7.8.** APP 7 does not apply to the extent that the Spam Act or DNCR Act applies. OAIC says APP 7 can still cover calls to numbers not on the DNCR, and other APPs can remain relevant where APP 7 is displaced. Document applicability by channel/cohort. Keep opt-out/source disclosure for every channel as a deliberate business standard. A YAML statement that consent is impracticable is evidence of an assessment, not proof that its conclusion is valid. | [OAIC: direct marketing](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/direct-marketing), [OAIC: APP text, 7.3 and 7.6–7.8](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/read-the-australian-privacy-principles) |
| SRC-07 | **The ACL rule is expressed too absolutely.** Business purchasers can qualify as consumers; it is not a general B2B exemption. But the specification must assess the relevant consumer definition, unsolicited negotiation circumstances, agreement value and exceptions, rather than conclude every services contract under $100,000 is automatically an unsolicited consumer agreement. ACCC explains unsolicited sale protections, a written agreement within five business days after a phone sale, and cooling-off rights. Model invitation evidence and agreement delivery/cooling-off dates; a boolean defaulting false is not a complete legal determination. Keep contracting/payment controls as a release gate for the downstream system. | [ACCC: business consumer rights](https://www.accc.gov.au/business/selling-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees), [ACCC: unsolicited sales](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/telemarketing-and-door-to-door-sales) |
| SRC-08 | **Weekly ABR publication is confirmed; exact Wednesday 08:33 schedule is not.** The ABN Lookup publisher now explicitly says weekly, so describing its cadence documentation as stale is unjustified. The specification's fixed local-time/DST explanation is an unverified observation and is internally awkward beside a Thursday 3 September member date. Keep metadata-driven discovery and matching-source-part checks. Do not encode a weekday/time or guaranteed snapshot member count. | [ABN Lookup: bulk extract](https://abr.business.gov.au/Tools/BulkExtract) |
| SRC-09 | **Automated domain/phrase heuristics must not be described as conclusive legal consent.** Own-domain publication, sole-trader status and an absence of matching phrases are useful evidence checks, but they cannot establish all legal facts by themselves. ACMA cautions that public publication alone is insufficient and that the sender must demonstrate consent. Require recorded assessment and allow an unknown outcome that blocks eligibility; retain the separate message/role relevance decision at send time. Email verification only assesses deliverability. | [ACMA: spam compliance alerts](https://www.acma.gov.au/spam-compliance-alerts), [ACMA: avoid sending spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam), [ACMA: dealing with spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/dealing-with-spam) |

## Verified safeguards worth retaining

### Additional source-schema check

The official [bulk-extract XSD](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/5bd7fcab-e315-42cb-8daf-50b7efc2027e/resource/f3f8f8a1-590c-4d87-8285-806b093c2c69/download/bulkextract.xsd)
was fetched as a small public schema on 2026-09-08. TransferInfo contains FileSequenceNumber
(integer), RecordCount (integer), ExtractTime (dateTime); Transfer requires an error string.
There is no generation UUID, total-member count or cross-part checksum. The
[publisher README](https://data.gov.au/data/dataset/5bd7fcab-e315-42cb-8daf-50b7efc2027e/resource/3b975e4f-af2f-4de7-a20e-6dcb3f67805c/download/abnlookupbulkextractreadme.pdf)
does not guarantee ExtractTime uniquely groups same-day corrections. Preserve its lexical timezone
information and require approved source mapping/evidence; hold uncertain groups. This verifies
schema documentation only: no personal-data ZIP was fetched or full live ingest executed.

- ABN Lookup lists status, type, names, state/postcode, ASIC identifiers, GST and DGR information as bulk fields. It does not list email, phone, website or street address. Collection and enrichment must remain separate, with provenance. [Publisher field list](https://abr.business.gov.au/Tools/BulkExtract).
- DNCR says washed results can generally be relied upon for 30 days. The pipeline should use a strict timestamp boundary, store the receipt and result, and repeat suppression/expiry checks when the contact is actually attempted. [Subscription overview](https://www.donotcall.gov.au/Industry/Subscription-Overview).
- ACMA independently requires sender identity/contact details and unsubscribe facilities; the latter must work for 30 days, avoid login/extra personal information and honour requests within five working days. Preserve all three duties: consent, identity, unsubscribe. [ACMA: avoid sending spam](https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam).
- APP 5 addresses notification at/before collection or as soon as practicable afterwards, subject to reasonable steps. “At first contact” is not automatically sufficient for indefinitely warehoused records. Record the notice strategy and its timing assessment. [APP 5 text](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/read-the-australian-privacy-principles).
