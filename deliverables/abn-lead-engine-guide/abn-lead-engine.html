The key idea

The engine helps the team decide which businesses are worth a closer look and what still needs checking before anything moves forward.

01

A research assistant
for the team.

Imagine starting with a very large list of businesses. Most will not be right for Maintain Media. Some will be unclear. A few may be worth investigating.

The ABN Lead Engine helps sort that list. It looks for businesses that match the chosen area and business type. It keeps the reason each business was found, helps check the business, and puts suitable records into a review process.

The purpose is to help Maintain Media find possible customers for its marketing services, with less repeated searching and a clearer reason for each decision.

A lead is a possibility. The business has not necessarily asked for help, agreed to be contacted or decided to buy anything.

What is an ABN?

An Australian Business Number: an 11-digit number used to identify a business. Think of it as a business reference number.

What is a lead?

A business that may be a suitable future customer. It still needs checking.

What does “engine” mean?

The software that works through the records, applies the rules and keeps the results organised.

Spend less time searching

Bring source records, findings and review notes together, so the team does not start from a blank page each time.

Know why a business appears

See the source, the possible fit, what is known and what is still missing.

Keep a shared memory

Remember earlier decisions, avoid repeated work and keep “do not contact” requests attached to the business.

Finding a business is only the beginning.This tool prepares research and review records. It does not send emails or texts, make calls, start campaigns, build customer websites or take payments.
02

Where the information
comes from.

The design has two main starting points. They answer different questions and must be checked in different ways.

ABR · Business register

“What changed in the business list?”

The Australian Business Register (ABR) provides a downloadable copy of public business details. It includes ABNs, names, registration information, and state and postcode.

How the engine uses it

Compare a complete earlier copy with a complete later copy. Look for newly observed active ABNs, GST registration changes, cancellations, reactivations and name changes. GST is a tax registration detail recorded for a business.

What it does not give you

A ready-made contact list. This extract does not supply the phone numbers and email addresses needed for outreach. A new observation also does not prove a business has just started trading.

QBCC · Contractor register

“Which contractors may fit?”

The Queensland Building and Construction Commission (QBCC) publishes a contractor register. The chosen starting group is financial Categories 1 and 2.

How the engine uses it

Keep a list of contractors to investigate. Check the current licence and the business identity before promoting a record into the next stage. A missing ABN is allowed; the licence can identify the source record.

What needs care

A financial category describes allowed revenue capacity. It does not tell us the business’s actual sales. An old register entry does not prove a licence is active today.

A website is a later source of evidence.After the business and website are checked, the approved website step can collect limited contact information. In the recorded pilot, that means phone numbers from reviewed business websites. Finding a number does not approve a call.

The whole system.
One connected picture.

Architecture means the parts of the system and how they connect. Data flow means where information moves. Follow the arrows from the team to the engine and its outputs.

Solid boxes are system parts. Dashed boxes show restricted research or review stages. Arrows describe the direction of information or requests.

The dashboard is the window.It shows the work and lets authorised staff request an action. The engine behind it checks that action again. A button on a screen cannot override a blocked record.

The engine is the worker and the memory.It does the background jobs and keeps their results. Private storage means work can be resumed while preserving earlier decisions and helping prevent duplicate transfers.

What is kept
about each business?

Think of a record as a small research folder. It contains more than a name and a number. It explains where the information came from and what can happen next.

  • Identity: “Which business is this?”

    Each record has an internal ID. ABNs, licence numbers and reviewed links help the engine recognise the same business across sources.

  • Evidence: “How do we know?”

    Keep the source, date, matched details and review evidence. Show old or missing information clearly. Unknown stays unknown.

  • Permission: “What may happen?”

    Keep contact checks, current restrictions and opt-outs separately from the score. A good fit can still be blocked.

  • History: “What happened already?”

    Keep decisions, job attempts, approvals and outcomes. Record who changed something and when. If two people edit an old version, the system flags the conflict.

Private evidence stays private.The system limits what appears in downloads and what can go to another service. A reviewer may need protected evidence to make a decision; that does not mean the full evidence should be copied into a spreadsheet or customer system.
06

How does it decide
what to look at first?

There are three separate questions: Does it fit? How useful is it to review? Is it allowed to move forward? A score only helps with the middle question.

A

Higher-priority signals

Eligible QBCC Category 1 or 2 candidates, or a newly observed GST registration for an active ABN whose active-status date is 12 to under 60 completed months old. Only selected, individually approved Tier A records may go to CRM.

B

Other potential-fit signals

A newly observed active ABN that is a company or trust, is GST-active and has a high or medium trade-name rule match. This is a separate group for review, not automatic CRM approval.

C

Counted, not selected as leads

Other newly observed ABNs. The engine counts them for understanding the source. Their appearance in a register is not enough to put them into the active lead list.

These are the specification’s selection rules. The ABR validation stage can test predictions privately; operational ABR selection still needs its own approval and evidence. An ABN active-status date is not the business’s birthday. Cancellations and name changes are also useful observations, but do not create fresh leads. A source cancellation blocks further lead work; an unexplained missing record is held for investigation. Reactivation does not remove a stop request.

Explain the score, with a simple example

The score is a sorting aid out of 100. It adds points for the source signal, location, business type and the strongest available contact evidence. It is not a percentage chance of buying.

QBCC example: Tier A 60 + Category 1 15 + company 5 + target area 10 + phone 15 = 105, capped at 100.

Only the strongest contact term counts: deliverable email 20, phone 15, website 5, or none 0. These are the wider design’s scoring terms; the narrow phone pilot does not activate email collection. Before contact evidence is added, the contact term is zero.

For ABR, the formula is Tier A 60 or B 30, plus trade-match confidence (high 15 or medium 5), target area 10, company 5 and the strongest contact term, capped at 100. The recovered trade rules look for words in business names; they can be wrong and need accuracy review.

Even a score of 100 cannot supply a missing check or permission. Ties are sorted by the earlier qualification date, then the internal record ID, so the order is repeatable.

08

What keeps the work
organised and controlled?

The visible list is only one part. The engine also needs to remember progress, handle problems and explain what happened.

A timer starts approved jobs.

A scheduler is a timer for background work. One scheduler controls when jobs start; saved locks prevent duplicate runs. Source checking can run on a weekly schedule when that collection is enabled. The current restricted pilot does not imply all weekly collection is running.

Each job has a saved place to resume.

A run records its steps and results. If a service is unavailable, unfinished work can wait or retry. An uncertain CRM transfer is checked before another create attempt, helping prevent duplicate contacts.

Spending has a ceiling.

The design includes a monthly enrichment limit of A$150, including the configured buffers and retries that cost money. That is a spending control, not permission to buy a service. A disabled paid service stays disabled.

Problems leave a visible reason.

Old source data, missing information, expired approvals, failed jobs and blocked exports appear in saved results and monitoring. “Nothing happened” should come with an explanation.

Information has a lifetime.

Retention means keeping information only for the allowed period. Cleanup jobs remove eligible expired material while respecting review holds. Backups need their own storage, expiry and restore checks.

Success is measured from real outcomes.

Useful measures include how many businesses pass review, how often contact information is usable, staff time spent and actual meetings. A large source file or a high score does not prove the tool is creating customers.

10

The questions people
usually ask first.

Open any question. Each answer stands on its own.

Will this tool automatically get us customers?

No. It helps prepare better research and review work. A business still needs to be a suitable fit, any contact must be separately permitted, and a person must choose to become a customer. Real outcomes are what show whether the tool is useful.

Does a new ABN mean a brand-new business?

No. It means the ABN was not in the earlier accepted copy and is active in the later one. The engine should say what it observed, not invent a business start date.

Does GST registration tell us how much a business earns?

No. The engine treats it as a research signal. It does not fill in a revenue figure or assume a sales threshold has been crossed.

Why can a business have a high score and still be blocked?

A score helps decide which eligible work to review first. Identity, permission, phone checks, current approvals and stop requests are separate. If one of those checks fails or is unknown, the score cannot override it.

Why are there fewer than 60 businesses on a list?

Sixty is the maximum. Some records may be duplicates, outside the target area, waiting for evidence, blocked, expired or not yet ready. The engine should show those reasons instead of filling the list with unsuitable records.

Is the local practice dashboard the real pilot?

No. The local dashboard uses invented practice businesses so the team can test workflows. The signed-in Maintain Media dashboard connects to the separate hosted service. Practice results are not evidence of real customer activity.

What does the team need to do each week?

Check run results and held records, review the current evidence, finish permitted checks, work through the eligible list and record decisions. If a record is blocked, resolve the reason or leave it held. Time savings must be measured; a two-hour week is a pilot idea, not a proven result.
