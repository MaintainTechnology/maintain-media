02

Paid media and lead generation

Maintain Media developed and launched a dedicated Meta lead-generation campaign for SIC, creating a direct-response channel designed to convert targeted advertising traffic into enquiries. The campaign has begun generating leads, establishing an initial performance baseline that can be developed further through continued optimisation, audience refinement and creative testing.

Advertising creative

To support campaign performance and give SIC multiple concepts to test, Maintain Media produced 12 dedicated advertising creatives. This gives the account a broader library of assets to rotate across different messages and visual approaches, rather than relying on a single concept, and creates more opportunities to identify the strongest-performing direction as campaign data develops.

Why multiple creative assets matter

  • Creates more opportunities to test different customer motivations and messages.
  • Reduces reliance on a single advertisement or visual direction.
  • Builds a reusable creative library for future campaign iterations.
  • Provides flexibility to refresh the campaign as performance data develops.
Key outcome

SIC now has an active paid acquisition channel on Meta, supported by a dedicated creative library and early lead generation.

04

Organic social media

Maintain Media developed social media content for SIC Surface, with the initial focus centred on asphalt driveways and resurfacing. The role of this content is to keep the brand active, provide useful context to prospective customers and support the credibility of paid advertising activity.

  • Showcases SIC Surface services and applications.
  • Demonstrates the visual impact of asphalt and resurfacing work.
  • Educates prospective customers around resurfacing options.
  • Keeps SIC Surface active and visible across social channels.
  • Supports familiarity and trust for prospects who research the business after seeing an ad.
  • Creates content that can be repurposed into future marketing and advertising assets.
Where it lands

Brand and creative work now spans three SIC business areas, from advertising design through to the branding on the physical product.
