# Website phone collection is live

Release 008 is running on the private Sydney engine. The requesting administrator
delegated the decision to activate a limited website-phone pilot without waiting
for Jon. Its scope is recorded separately from the earlier QBCC source decision.
It does not claim an adviser assessment or permission to contact a business.

The first real website run completed on 11 September 2026 at 00:50:15 UTC
(08:50 Manila). One genuinely reviewed QBCC business now has **two office phone
records**, with encrypted page evidence. The engine read four pages using six
requests, respected its robots and same-domain controls, and extracted no email
contacts. Both private evidence views were checked against the reviewed website.

These are real collected records. They are still **awaiting permission review**;
there are no selected worklist rows or approved exports. No calls, messages,
Google Sheet pulls or GoHighLevel contact transfers were made.

## Use it

1. Open the [ABN Lead Gen dashboard](https://www.maintainmedia.com.au/abn-lead-gen/dashboard).
2. Sign in with the approved Maintain Media account and refresh the page.
3. Open the reviewed business, then **Review phone evidence → Open private
   captured evidence** to see the saved website evidence.
4. For another business, complete its current QBCC licence review and website
   identity review. Open **Collect phone details from the reviewed website**,
   enter the genuine site-terms review, then select **Collect reviewed phone details**.

The engine saves the request and result. A current licence, matching website,
actual terms review and runtime source controls are required for each business.
Finding a number is not permission to call it. Only mobile and actual landline
numbers are in this pilot; email extraction and 1300/1800 collection are excluded.

## Dates and controls

Manual QBCC and website collection expire on **24 September 2026 at 23:18:20 UTC**
(25 September, 07:18 Manila). There is no automatic renewal or bulk website crawl.
Deletion authority continues separately until 10 September 2027 at 23:18:20 UTC.

Queued encrypted requests expire after 24 hours and are removed on completion.
Ordinary page evidence expires after 90 days; unworked profiles after 180 days
from their last qualifying event, with applicable 30-day restriction minimisation.
This pilot disables the seven-year selected-evidence archive. The real retention
service completed successfully at 00:54:18 UTC, with both new encrypted records
preserved, zero queued inputs and zero archives. Future expiry and disaster
recovery have not been certified by this fresh-data check.

The [business research notice](https://www.maintainmedia.com.au/business-research-notice)
is published and verified. It explains the sources, purpose, retention, request
contact and processing limitations without claiming individual notification or
legal certification.
