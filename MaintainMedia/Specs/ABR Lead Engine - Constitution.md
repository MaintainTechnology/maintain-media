# Maintain Media Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Evidence before claims
Requirements MUST distinguish observed data, verified documentation, estimates and assumptions.
A tax registration, licence category or public email MUST NOT be presented as proof of revenue,
purchase intent or consent. Reported tests MUST have results tied to a revision and environment.

### II. Permission and suppression are authoritative
Personal information MUST have a recorded purpose, provenance, finite retention and restricted
access. Unknown eligibility MUST block contact. Suppression MUST be checked at action time,
survive deletion and restoration, and propagate across matching identities. Cached spreadsheet
labels MUST NOT authorise sending. Production use requires policy, account and release gates;
synthetic development can proceed without them.

### III. Deterministic and recoverable data
Publications MUST have immutable content identities. Partial or inconsistent sources MUST NOT
become the baseline. State promotion MUST be transactional and replayable. Classification,
ranking and spend accounting MUST have deterministic rules and durable failure recovery.

### IV. Small, bounded operations
Prefer one Python application, a CLI, one relational authority and analytical snapshot files.
Infrastructure requires a documented purpose. External calls MUST have timeouts, budget
reservations and bounded retries. Measure costs, capacity and human work before expanding.

### V. Traceable acceptance
Every requirement MUST map to a task and measurable acceptance evidence. Security and integrity
tests MUST cover permitted and rejected cases. Passing documentation checks does not establish
that software exists, production works or legal review occurred.

## Scope and Constraints
These principles govern Maintain Media engineering specifications. ABR Part 1 covers
qualification and controlled worklists; messaging, dialling and client delivery are separate.
Preserve unrelated brand assets and the established design system. Use Australian English.

## Development Workflow and Quality Gates
Use specification, clarification, plan, requirements checklist, tasks and consistency analysis.
Implement reviewable slices with synthetic fixtures first. Record source and release approvals
separately from technical completion. Convergence MUST leave unimplemented tasks unchecked.
No release follows from a score alone.

## Governance
The constitution governs feature plans; explicit user instructions remain authoritative.
Amendments require a recorded reason, impact review and semantic version change. Legal policy,
source permissions and launch scope require the responsible owner's recorded decision.
Reviewers MUST identify conflicts rather than silently choose an interpretation.
