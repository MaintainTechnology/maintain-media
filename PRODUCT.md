# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Maintain Media's internal team, designers, marketers, and contractors who produce branded
material — infographics, documents, decks, social graphics, and website features. Their
context is mid-build: they are reaching for the correct logo, colour, icon, or font while
making something, and they need one authoritative reference so every deliverable ships
on-brand without guesswork.

## Product Purpose

The Maintain Media Design System is the single source of truth for the brand's visual
identity. It catalogues and specifies every brand asset — logos, colours, typography,
iconography, graphics, and backgrounds — and links directly to the source files in
`media/`. Success looks like: anyone building a Maintain Media visual finds the exact asset
and its spec here first, copies a hex or downloads a logo in seconds, and never guesses a
colour value, font, or logo variant again.

## Brand Personality

Modern, professional, tech-forward. Confident and precise without being cold — a software
company's polish: purple energy on deep near-black, clean geometric sans typography, and a
distinctive ascending "M" mountain mark. The voice is clear and direct, never salesy, cute,
or ornamental.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI / SaaS template look** — cream backgrounds, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow
  labels above every section, endless identical icon-heading-text card grids.
- **Cluttered, busy layouts** with no breathing room.
- **Corporate-stiff, lifeless** stock-corporate feel.
- **Childish or overly playful** treatments.

## Design Principles

- **Single source of truth.** If a spec isn't here, it isn't official. Every asset the brand
  owns is shown and linked, not merely described.
- **Show the real asset.** The page renders the actual files — logos, icons, fonts,
  gradients — never a redrawn approximation. It is wired to `media/`.
- **Identity preservation.** The brand already exists (purple `#a04dff`, dark `#08282d`, the
  mountain "M"). Document and enforce it; do not reinvent it.
- **Usable under pressure.** Someone mid-build should copy a hex, grab a logo, or confirm a
  font in seconds — copy-to-clipboard, download links, clear do / don't.
- **On-brand by example.** The design-system page itself must embody the brand it documents.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥ 3:1. Full keyboard support, visible
focus states, and a `prefers-reduced-motion` path for every animation. Colour is never the
only signal — every swatch carries its name and hex, every icon its label.
